How MAYA works

MAYA scans your watchlist every 60 seconds and scores each stock on 3 indicators:

EMA Crossover
Fast (9-bar) EMA crosses above Slow (21-bar) EMA — signals upward momentum
40 points max
RSI Zone
RSI between 35-45 is the sweet spot — not overbought, not oversold
35 points max
MACD Momentum
Histogram positive and MACD line above zero — confirms the trend
25 points max

A trade fires only when all 5 conditions are met: regime, confidence ≥ 65%, MA crossover, RSI in range, and daily trade limit available.
